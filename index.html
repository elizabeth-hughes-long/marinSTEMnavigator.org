National & Competitive Programs

Summer Programs Worth Traveling For

Service academy previews, federal agency programs, and national competitions. You are selected on what you can do, not on what you can pay. Several charge a participation or travel fee, listed on each card, and most offer need-based assistance.

Selective, not for sale. These are not the $3,000 “leadership academies” advertised in college prep magazines, where a deposit is the only entrance requirement. Every one is run by the U.S. government, a federal service academy, or a recognized research institution, and admission is decided on merit. Some still cost money to attend. Check each card.
Application timing matters. Most of these open in December or January for the following summer. Set a reminder in November to check each program’s site. Missing the window by a week is the most common way students lose these.
